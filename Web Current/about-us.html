About Outside the Box Education

Learning was never meant to be one-size-fits-all.

Every child is born with unique strengths, interests, questions, and ways of understanding the world. But too often, children are measured by a system designed for averages instead of supported as individuals.

Outside the Box Education exists to help families discover a different way. We believe education should begin with the child — not the curriculum, the test, or the system.

Our Story

What if struggling learners aren't failing?

Outside the Box Education began with a simple question: what if the way we are teaching kids simply isn't the way they learn best?

After years as an elementary teacher, curriculum writer, and homeschool educator, founder Mindi Eldredge saw the same pattern again and again: children who were capable, creative, and curious were losing confidence because they didn't fit into a traditional educational model.

When her own children faced learning challenges, she discovered the power of looking beyond "catching up" and instead focusing on understanding how each child learns best.

That experience became the foundation for Outside the Box Education — a place where families can find encouragement, research, resources, and real stories from parents creating meaningful educational paths for their children.

Meet the Founder

Mindi Eldredge

Mindi Eldredge is an educator, author, curriculum developer, homeschool advocate, and mother of three children ages 13–20, with a passion for helping children learn in ways that honor their individuality.

With a background in elementary education, years of experience creating curriculum, and over a decade of hands-on experience homeschooling her own children, Mindi has dedicated her work to helping families move beyond traditional expectations and discover educational approaches that allow children to thrive.

Through Outside the Box Education and The Mindful Heart, she creates resources designed to encourage parents, support learners, and make meaningful, accessible education available to families everywhere.

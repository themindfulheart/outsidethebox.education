Know your options

A few different ways families get help paying for it.

Funding for personalized education generally falls into a handful of buckets. Most states offer at least one — many now offer several.

🐖

Education Savings Accounts

State-deposited funds parents can direct toward curriculum, tutoring, therapies, testing, and more.

🎟️

Vouchers & tuition grants

State funding that follows a student to a private, charter, or alternative school of the family's choice.

🎓

Scholarships & tax credits

Privately funded scholarships, often supported by state tax-credit programs, covering tuition or enrichment.

📚

Free resources & microgrants

Library co-op passes, umbrella school stipends, and small grants for curriculum, field trips, and extracurriculars.

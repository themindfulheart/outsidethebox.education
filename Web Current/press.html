ABOUT THE PLATFORM

A trusted voice in homeschooling

Outside the Box Education is an educational media and resource platform created to help families discover learning approaches that honor each child's unique needs, strengths, interests, and way of learning.

Through videos, podcasts, articles, guides, curriculum resources, and real-life stories, Outside the Box Education explores homeschooling, alternative education, child development, learning differences, teaching methods, and the many ways education can look beyond the traditional classroom.

Our mission is to help parents move beyond one-size-fits-all expectations and feel more informed, supported, and confident as they create an education that works for their children and their families.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Mindi Eldredge

Mindi Eldredge

Educator · Author · Curriculum Creator · Founder, Outside the Box Education & The Mindful Heart

Mindi Eldredge is an educator, author, curriculum creator, homeschool advocate, and founder of Outside the Box Education and The Mindful Heart.

With a background in elementary education and years of experience creating educational resources, Mindi has dedicated her work to helping families discover learning approaches that honor each child's individual strengths, needs, and unique way of learning.

Before founding Outside the Box Education, Mindi led a curriculum development team that was contracted by The Good and the Beautiful to create the original K–4 math courses, serving as the creator and writer of those courses. Her curriculum development work also included creating additional educational resources and science units.

Throughout her career, Mindi has focused on helping parents move beyond one-size-fits-all education and recognize that children learn differently. Her work explores personalized learning, learning differences, homeschool methods, child development, curriculum choices, and practical ways families can create meaningful educational experiences.

As a mother of three children and a longtime homeschool advocate, Mindi brings both professional expertise and personal experience to conversations about education. Through Outside the Box Education, she shares resources, stories, and encouragement to help families think differently about what education can look like.

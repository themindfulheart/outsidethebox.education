The Outside the Box Education Podcast

Every homeschooling journey begins with a story.

The Outside the Box Education Podcast shares the real experiences of families who chose a different path for their children’s education. Through honest conversations and personal stories, we explore why families left traditional schools, how they discovered new ways of learning, and the challenges and triumphs that shaped their journey.

This podcast goes beyond typical homeschool interviews. These are stories of children who didn’t fit the system, parents who searched for answers, and families who found confidence, connection, and a love of learning outside the box.

You’ll hear stories about homeschooling children with unique learning needs, teaching multiple ages, discovering unexpected strengths, overcoming struggles, and creating an education that truly fits each child. Whether you are new to homeschooling, considering a different educational path, or simply looking for inspiration, Outside the Box Education Podcast reminds us that every child learns differently—and every family has a story worth sharing.

Because sometimes the greatest transformations happen when we stop asking, “How do we make a child fit the system?” and start asking, “How can we create a system that fits the child?”
